Our Story

From Mill to Hotel

Casa Ulika has pressed olives, milled grain, and now houses travellers — always on the same stretch of coastline, four generations of the same family.

A family business, still

The mill was built in 1887 by the Vuković family to press olives from the surrounding groves. It operated for over a century before closing in the 1990s, when cheaper imported oil made small mills unprofitable across the region.

In 2017, the founder's great-granddaughter, Ana Vuković, returned to Vrisnik with a plan to restore the building rather than sell it. Casa Ulika opened in 2019 with twelve rooms, keeping the original stone press as a centrepiece in the courtyard.

The grove is still harvested every November, and the hotel's breakfast oil comes from those same two hundred trees.

Timeline

136 years on the same stone

  1. 1887

    The mill is built

    Ivan Vuković constructs the olive mill using stone quarried from the hillside directly behind the building.

  2. 1962

    Second press installed

    A larger stone press is added to meet demand from neighbouring groves, doubling the mill's output.

  3. 1994

    The mill closes

    Rising costs and cheaper imported oil force the mill to close. The building is used for storage for two decades.

  4. 2017

    Restoration begins

    Ana Vuković returns to Vrisnik and begins an 18-month restoration, working with local stonemasons to keep the original walls intact.

  5. 2019

    Casa Ulika opens

    The hotel opens with twelve rooms and a restored courtyard, with the original press kept as a working exhibit.

Our Approach

What we changed, and what we kept

Kept: the walls

Every exterior wall is original 1887 stonework. Restoration added insulation from the inside so the facade needed no repointing.

Changed: the roof

The original timber roof had rotted beyond repair. It was rebuilt using the same beam spacing, in reclaimed oak from a mill two villages over.

Kept: the grove

All two hundred trees remain in production. Guests are welcome to walk the grove at any time during their stay.
